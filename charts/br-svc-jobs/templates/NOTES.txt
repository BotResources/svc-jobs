NATS at {{ .Values.nats.url }} must already carry the declared JetStream
topology — streams INTEGRATION_CMD, INTEGRATION_EVT, JOBS_TRIGGER, JOBS_STATUS,
JOBS_LOG and KV buckets JOBS_CANCEL, JOBS_PRESENCE and PUBLISHED_LANGUAGE.
svc-jobs binds them and
fails the boot loudly when one is absent; it never creates them. Keep the
JOBS_CANCEL bucket's TTL at least as long as the longest maximum run duration
any runner type is set to (a new type starts at JOBS_RUN_MAX_DURATION_SECONDS,
default 72h). Instances of CLAIM runner types request their runs on
jobs.claim.v2.{runner_type}, and every runner instance sends its requests to
jobs.runner.{started,register_agents,list_agents,mark_read}.{runner_type} (core
NATS request/reply): the runner credentials must be allowed to publish on
jobs.claim.v2.* and jobs.runner.*.* and to subscribe to their reply inboxes, and
svc-jobs to subscribe to the same subjects and to publish the replies.
svc-jobs must also be allowed to subscribe to
$JS.EVENT.ADVISORY.CONSUMER.MSG_TERMINATED.JOBS_TRIGGER.> (a run whose trigger a runner
terminates is failed from that broker advisory); runner credentials must not be
allowed to publish on $JS.EVENT.ADVISORY.>. Without the right the server refuses
the subscription silently: no run is failed by a termination, the pod stays ready,
and the refusal shows as a warn line and in jobs_nats_server_errors_total.

Retention: a finished Tree is kept {{ .Values.retention.days }} day(s) after its last activity
(JOBS_RETENTION_DAYS, 1 to 395); the request data of a cancelled Job is kept
{{ .Values.retention.cancelledDays }} day(s) after the cancel (JOBS_CANCELLED_RETENTION_DAYS, 0 to 395, never
above the first). svc-jobs refuses to boot outside these bounds.
